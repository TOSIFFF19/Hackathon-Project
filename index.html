Each interest dimension averages the ratings (1–5) of statements mapped to it, then converts that mean using ((mean − 1) ÷ 4) × 100. Neutral maps to 50, not a weakness. Career scores average the mapped dimension percentages; when profile tags are comparable, the score is 70% quiz and 30% profile overlap. The profile component compares provided interests, skills, subjects, and goals with tags in each career profile. These are exploratory preferences—not ability measures or success odds. Career titles describe fields or occupations, not degree programs, entrance exams, or qualifications.
